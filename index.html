THE STORY OF COACH

The story of Coach is a testament to the brand's enduring legacy and its commitment to quality and authenticity. Founded in 1941, Coach began as a family-run workshop in Manhattan and quickly evolved into a global fashion house. The brand's journey is marked by significant milestones, including the introduction of the iconic "turnlock" hardware and the expansion of its product offerings to include bags, wallets, and more. Coach's creative direction has been led by visionary figures like Bonnie Cashin and Stuart Vevers, who have redefined the brand's identity and appeal. Today, Coach continues to inspire with collections that reflect the spirit of New York and the courage to be real.
